About Us

Learn more about Ashley's Sewing Company.

Our History

Ashley's Sewing Company is a small sewing business that provides professional clothing design, sewing, alterations and repair services. The business was created to provide customers with quality and affordable clothing made according to their individual needs.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality, stylish and affordable sewing services while meeting the unique needs of every customer.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become a trusted sewing company known for creativity, quality workmanship and excellent customer service.

Our Target Customers

  • Individuals who need custom-made clothing
  • Customers requiring alterations
  • Schools requiring uniforms
  • Businesses and organisations
  • Customers looking for unique clothing designs
